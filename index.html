FAQ

Questions people ask us

How do payments work?

You get a fixed written quote first. To start, you pay a 50% deposit through our Yoco page. When your site is finished, you pay the other 50% and we put it live. Hosting is R150 a month after the first month.

Do you add VAT?

No. The prices on this page are the full amount you pay.

What do I need to give you?

Your logo (if you have one), a few lines about your business, your services, the areas you work in, your contact details and photos of your work. If writing isn't your thing, we can write the text for you for R300 a page.

How long does it take?

It mostly depends on how quickly we receive your text and photos. We'll give you a target date in your written quote.

Can you fix my current website instead of making a new one?

Often, yes. Our fix-up starts from R1,450. We look at your site first and confirm a fixed price before any work starts. If a fix isn't worth your money, we'll tell you and quote a new site instead.

Will I be on the first page of Google?

Nobody can honestly promise that. We set your site up properly so Google can read it, and our Local SEO setup helps people in your area find you, but rankings depend on many things outside our control.

Is there a contract?

The website build is a once-off payment. Hosting and the optional care plan are monthly and can be cancelled with one month's notice.

Do you only work in Pretoria and Centurion?

That's where we focus, but most of the work happens over email and WhatsApp, so ask us if you're somewhere else in South Africa.
