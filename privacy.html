Privacy notice

Short version: we only use your details to reply to you about your website. We don't sell them or share them for marketing.

Who we are

Skip Digital SA builds websites for local businesses in Pretoria and Centurion, South Africa. You can reach us at skipdigitalsa@gmail.com or on 082 068 9422.

What the quote form collects

  • Your name and business name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone or WhatsApp number, if you choose to give it
  • Your current website address, if you have one
  • The package you're interested in and your message

Why we collect it

Only to reply to your enquiry, prepare your quote, and talk to you about your website if you go ahead. This follows the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA): we collect only what we need, for this purpose only.

Who else sees it

  • The form is delivered to our email inbox by a form service called FormSubmit, and our email is hosted by Google (Gmail).
  • If you pay us, the payment is handled by Yoco. We don't see or store your card details.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your details with anyone for marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep your enquiry only for as long as we need it to quote, do the work and keep normal business records. If you don't go ahead, you can ask us to delete it at any time.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Just email skipdigitalsa@gmail.com. If you're unhappy with how we handled your information, you may complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Our website host may keep standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) to keep the site running and secure.
